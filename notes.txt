imdlib — downloads and parses IMD's gridded data directly into Python objects

xarray + netCDF4 + dask — the standard toolkit for NetCDF climate data; dask enables lazy loading so 124 years don't blow up your RAM

scipy + statsmodels — SARIMAX, exponential smoothing, statistical tests

scikit-learn — Ridge/Lasso, metrics, walk-forward cross-validation

geopandas + regionmask — masking the grid into IMD's subdivisions

tqdm — progress bars for those long downloads
